My AdGuard experience

AdGuard has really been great, but there's just been one problem: Is it worth the price?
Short answer, most likely yes! There are a couple of caveats, but I believe that it's better than having nothing, and something having caveats is normal. That's life.

Just starting off, and AdGuard already gives you a 14 day trial, just by registering an account!
That's superior and easy to do, and you can decide if you can buy it later.

They offer browser extensions and apps, and I would go over apps, since they block ads and trackers system-wide instead of browser-wide.

The protection is actually excellent for an adblocker! You might have issues and all that, but as I said earlier, nothing in life is 100% perfect. The AdGuard team really created a great adblocker and tracker blocker!

They also block many other disgusting things, like cookie pop-ups. It's there, but some sites don't allow it, so it's at your own risk.

I've been using it, and it's great overall! The only thing is it's paid, but for such a price, it's OK.

Do I recommend it? Yes!
Does this mean that AdGuard is the best? That's for you to decide! That is your opinion.

Seeing the internet with ads makes me feel drunk (sarcasm), mostly because I have used other adblockers, like uBlock Origin for years.

Removing ads is an essential part of the Internet now. We sadly can't stop it, but we can work together to make the Internet comfortable to use.

Bye, and I hope nothing will ever stop anyone from accessing data on the Internet.
